You didn't become a tutor to write emails. But between parent updates, session recaps, progress reports, and the occasional hard conversation, that's where a real share of your week goes — not at the table with your student.

The ClassStack AI Toolkit is 10 prompts, built specifically for independent tutors and private teachers, that turn a blank page into a finished draft in under a minute:

- New student intake recap
- Session recap for parents
- Lesson plan generator
- Difficult conversation with a parent
- End-of-term progress report

- Referral request to a satisfied parent
- Social media caption batch
- Homework assignment with parent instructions

Each prompt works with any AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, or similar) — copy it in, fill in your real details, and you have a first draft ready before you've packed up your materials. **Who this is not for:** tutors who want an AI to make judgment calls about a student for them. This toolkit drafts. You still sign off on everything that reaches a parent.
